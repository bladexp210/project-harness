# Acme Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

This document is the single approved source of truth for Acme Paid Time Off rules.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 15 days (120 hours) of paid time off per calendar year, credited at 1.25 days on the last business day of each month. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis proportional to their scheduled weekly hours. New hires begin accruing PTO on their first day of employment, and accrued days are available for use immediately upon credit.

## Notice

Employees must submit PTO requests in the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for absences of 1 to 3 consecutive business days. For absences of 4 or more consecutive business days, employees must submit requests at least 10 business days in advance. Same-day or next-day PTO requests for unexpected personal emergencies require direct manager notification in Slack before 9:30 AM local time.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days (40 hours) into the following calendar year. Any unused PTO above the 5-day carryover cap expires on December 31 and is not paid out as cash at year-end. Carried-over PTO days must be used by March 31 of the new calendar year or they expire automatically.
